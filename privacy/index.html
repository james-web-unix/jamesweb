Privacy Policy

James Web has no accounts, no forms, no ads and no analytics. This page lists what is stored in your browser and which third-party services the site loads.

Written by AR. Abhinav Ranjan · Last reviewed 3 October 2026

In short

This website has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no comments and no advertising. It does not currently load any analytics or tracking scripts. We do not collect your name, email address or other personal details through this site.

What is stored in your browser

Some features save small items in your own browser using local storage. They never leave your device and are not sent to us.

  • Reader: your bookmarks and recently read books.
  • Legal Lab Checklist: which boxes you have ticked on the James Web Logical page.
  • Offline support: a service worker keeps copies of site files so the site can show an offline page.

You can remove all of this by clearing site data for jamesweb.dpdns.org in your browser settings.

Third-party services this site loads

ServiceUsed for
Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)Typefaces. Your browser contacts Google to download them, which shares your IP address with Google.
cdnjs by Cloudflare (cdnjs.cloudflare.com)The PDF.js library that displays books in the reader, and Font Awesome icons.
Netlify (hosting)Serves the site. Hosting providers typically keep server logs such as IP address, time and requested page.
Amazon and Google PlayOnly if you click a store button. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the site does not collect it.

Changes

If this changes, for example if analytics are added, this page will be updated first and the change recorded in the log. Questions: Contact.
