Permission comes before practice

Everything James Web publishes follows one rule: test only what you own or are explicitly authorized to test. The Legal Lab Checklist turns that rule into five checks you complete before any session.

  1. Confirm permission and scope. Make sure you have explicit authorization for the specific target and testing activity.
  2. Set target boundaries. Write down exactly which systems, IP addresses or domains are in scope.
  3. Define the session goal. State what you are trying to learn or verify before you begin.
  4. Set stop conditions. Decide in advance when testing must stop.
  5. Keep evidence notes. Record what you tested, what happened and what still needs verification.

Frequently Asked

What is James Web?

James Web is a division of Luminary Technicals, founded by AR. Abhinav Ranjan, focused on legal ethical-hacking learning and research. It publishes the James Series of books, starting with James Web Logical, and offers a free online reader.

Is James Web related to the James Webb Space Telescope?

No. James Web is not affiliated with NASA, ESA, CSA, or the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) in any way. The similar name is a coincidence. James Web is a cybersecurity and ethical hacking education brand.

Who runs James Web?

James Web is built and maintained by AR. Abhinav Ranjan, founder of Luminary Technicals, as part of a wider ecosystem of educational and technical projects.

Does James Web teach illegal hacking?

No. Every James Web lesson follows a permission-first model: you test only systems you own or have explicit written authorization to test, within a defined scope, with stop conditions and written notes. See the Ethics & Responsible Use policy for the full rules.

Is James Web Logical free?

The online edition is free to read in India with no login. Outside India, the Kindle edition on Amazon and the Google Play Books edition are listed at $3 USD.

Where can I get James Web Logical?

Read it online at jamesweb.dpdns.org/logical/ and in the James Web reader, or get the Kindle edition on Amazon and the edition on Google Play Books using the buttons on this site.
